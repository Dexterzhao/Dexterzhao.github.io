SEEKING A COMPUTER GRAPHICS PhD

From building games

I am Ziang Zhao, a game software engineer and educator working toward research in real-time graphics, game engine rendering, procedural terrain generation, and 3D game asset generation.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My background spans approximately five years in the game industry, including Tencent, Huanle Entertainment, NetEase, and vivo. I now teach Computer Science and Physics at St Bees School in Dongguan.

Looking ahead

I am seeking PhD opportunities in Computer Graphics, with particular interest in the Greater Bay Area, including HKUST (Guangzhou), The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and nearby research groups. I would welcome conversations with potential supervisors about research fit and preparation for doctoral study.

01 / RESEARCH DIRECTION

Controllable worlds.
Interactive performance.

How can learned generation become a useful part of an editable, reproducible, real-time world-building workflow?

Game engine rendering

Understand the interaction between scene representation, shading, visibility, level of detail, and the performance budgets of interactive applications.

Procedural terrain

Explore generative terrain with explicit structural controls, coherent large-scale geography, and predictable behavior across seeds and query locations.

3D assets & environments

Study how generated assets, materials, and vegetation can fit terrain, respect artistic edits, and remain practical to render and stream.

Current explorationIndependent study · Replication-oriented

Learning from InfiniteDiffusion / Terrain Diffusion

I am exploring Alexander Goslin's InfiniteDiffusion: Bridging Learned Fidelity and Procedural Utility for Open-World Terrain Generation (SIGGRAPH 2026) as a starting point for a terrain-generation research project. My immediate focus is understanding the paper and public implementation, planning baseline replication, and defining a measurable evaluation protocol.

InfiniteDiffusion and Terrain Diffusion are Goslin's work. This is an independent exploration; no completed replication, collaboration, or new research result is claimed here.

What I bring: production experience with C++/Lua/Python, Linux builds and CI/CD, profiling, automated testing, and multiplayer systems. These support reliable experiments and engine integration. My industry roles were primarily backend, gameplay, and test engineering; I am developing my graphics and rendering expertise through focused study and experimentation.
