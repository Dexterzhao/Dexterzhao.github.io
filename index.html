Graphics interests

I am studying rendering and geometry alongside generative methods for game environments. The topics below describe my current interests and questions for further investigation.

Real-time rendering & GPU programming

Rendering pipelines, physically based shading, image-based lighting, shadows, and global illumination. I am interested in temporal reconstruction, GPU-driven visibility and level of detail, and how shader execution and memory use affect frame time.

3D representations & neural rendering

Meshes, signed distance fields, neural radiance fields (NeRF), and 3D Gaussian Splatting. My interests include the tradeoffs between rendering cost, memory, editability, and the separation of geometry, materials, and lighting.

Procedural terrain generation

Seed-consistent generation, multi-scale terrain structure, ridge and river constraints, and local editing. I want to examine how these properties behave when terrain is generated and streamed in a game engine.

3D assets for game engines

Generated geometry, textures, and materials, with attention to topology, UVs, level-of-detail generation, collision, and engine integration. A particular question is how to retain useful controls and editable representations throughout the asset pipeline.

Current explorationPaper and code study

Terrain generation study: InfiniteDiffusion / Terrain Diffusion

I am exploring Alexander Goslin's InfiniteDiffusion: Bridging Learned Fidelity and Procedural Utility for Open-World Terrain Generation (SIGGRAPH 2026) through its paper and public implementation. Baseline replication, terrain controls, and evaluation are the next steps in this study.

InfiniteDiffusion and Terrain Diffusion are Goslin's published work. My study is independent; baseline replication and the experiments described in the research notes are planned.
